Biography · Key Milestones

2003 — The Beginning

First steps into hip‑hop culture. The era of the Hip-Hop.ru forums, where the foundation of style and the honest word was laid. The archives of that time became the DNA of the TAYMAY project.

The Journey

Years of creative exploration, sharpening the author’s pen, and building deep meanings behind every track. Music as digital concrete — solid, authentic, and timeless.

Today

TAYMAY (Roman Alekseevich Ryabko) is more than just sound — it is a mission. A fusion of old‑school foundations with modern technology and social responsibility.

“These aren't just beats; this is the pulse of generations, frozen in digital form.”
